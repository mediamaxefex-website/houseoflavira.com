01 — THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

Elegance that feels natural.

HOUSE OF LAVIRA is a fashion brand based in Kavalam, Kerala. Our inspiration is simple: style should feel as comfortable and individual as the person wearing it.

Drawing from the calm landscapes of Kuttanad and a contemporary approach to fashion, we love considered palettes, timeless details and looks that speak quietly yet confidently.

We believe the most beautiful thing you can wear is a sense of belonging to yourself.
